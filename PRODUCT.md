# Avida Dental

A Persian, right-to-left dental practice website. The public homepage helps visitors and families understand the available services, prepare for a first visit, and know what they can ask during the consultation. The site also includes signed-in notes and account flows.

Treat the service directory and the visit process already described on the site as the source for clinic claims. Represent generated imagery as illustrative. The homepage has an on-page first-visit guide as its primary action.

The repository uses TanStack Start, Tailwind, Paraglide, and Vazirmatn. See `docs/frontend-flow.md` for implementation conventions and `docs/frontend/app-identity.md` for brand and accessibility guidance.
