# Writing Guide — Avida Dental Manual (Cisco Press Style)

Every chapter follows the Cisco Press Official Guide structure adapted for Persian RTL readers:

1. **Chapter Opener & Objectives (`> [!features]`):** Large chapter badge beside the title, heavy rule, and concise bullet list of what the reader accomplishes («در این فصل»).
2. **Task-Oriented Sections (`##`):** Ordered exactly as the clinic team performs them, with hanging numbered steps (`گام ۱`, `گام ۲`, ...), exact on-screen labels in `[[…]]`, and figure callout numbers in `{1}`, `{2}`, ...
3. **Numbered Figures & Reference Tables:** Every screenshot (`![shot-id]`) is numbered by chapter («شکل ۳-۱»), and every status/mode/troubleshooting reference (`[!table]`) is numbered («جدول ۳-۱») with a shaded header and hairline horizontal rules.
4. **Cisco Key Topics (`[!key]`):** 3 to 6 critical facts, irreversible actions, or operational rules per chapter marked with `[!key] <short title>`. The build adds the margin Key Topic badge and compiles the end-of-chapter **«مرور نکته‌های کلیدی»** review table automatically.
5. **Notes, Operational Tips & Warnings:** `> [!note]`, `> [!tip]`, and `> [!warn]` set between two crisp horizontal rules with inline icons.
6. **Troubleshooting Table («رفع مشکل»):** Always the final authored section of every chapter, formatted as a two-column table: **«آنچه می‌بینید»** and **«چه باید کرد»**.
